# Thesis AI Naturalizer

English | [한국어](#한국어) | [中文](#中文)

## English

Thesis AI Naturalizer is an AI-agent skill for Library and Information Science (LIS) thesis writing and revision. It supports literature-grounded drafting, thesis paragraph generation, and CopyKiller report-guided academic prose revision.

The skill is designed to effectively reduce AI-like writing patterns and AI-detection risk by improving evidence use, paragraph logic, field-specific context, and natural academic expression. It does not fabricate citations, distort research data, or guarantee a fixed detector score.

## Features

- CopyKiller report-guided revision for high-risk thesis paragraphs
- LIS-focused literature search, reading, and synthesis workflow
- Literature-grounded thesis drafting and paragraph rewriting
- Korean and bilingual academic writing support
- Citation-safety rules to avoid fake or unsupported references
- Private customization for other fields, topics, and writing standards

## Use Cases

- Revise mechanical or template-like thesis prose
- Improve literature review sections with verified sources
- Generate thesis paragraphs from real literature and user-provided data
- Map CopyKiller report markers back to the current draft
- Adapt the workflow to another discipline by replacing field-specific references and terms

## How To Use

For Codex or compatible AI agents:

1. Place this folder in your skills directory.
2. Ask the agent to use `thesis-ai-naturalizer`.
3. Provide your thesis draft, CopyKiller report, research topic, data, and references when available.
4. Review all generated citations and factual claims before submission.

For other agents:

1. Ask the agent to read `SKILL.md`.
2. Ask it to follow the referenced workflows in `references/`.
3. Provide your own literature, data, and formatting rules.

## Customization

This skill can be privately customized for other disciplines or thesis topics:

- replace LIS anchors with your field's terminology
- replace `references/verified_literature.md` with your checked source pool
- adjust Korean or bilingual style rules
- add discipline-specific risk patterns from your own revision experience

## Academic Integrity

This project is intended for legitimate academic writing support. It should be used to improve clarity, evidence, structure, and scholarly expression. It should not be used to invent sources, fake data, or misrepresent research results.

## 中文

Thesis AI Naturalizer 是一个面向 AI Agent 的论文写作与修订 skill，主要服务于图书馆与信息科学（Library and Information Science, LIS）领域。它支持文献支撑写作、论文段落生成，以及基于 CopyKiller 报告的学术文本自然化修订。

这个 skill 的目标是通过加强文献依据、段落逻辑、领域语境和自然学术表达，有效降低文本 AI 化表达和 AI 检测风险。它不编造引用，不篡改研究数据，也不保证固定检测分数。

## 功能

- 根据 CopyKiller 报告定位并修订高风险论文段落
- 面向图书馆与信息科学领域的文献检索、阅读和综述整理流程
- 基于真实文献和用户材料生成或改写论文内容
- 支持韩文及中韩/英韩双语学术写作场景
- 提供引用安全规则，避免虚假或不支撑论点的参考文献
- 支持私人化定制，可迁移到其他学科、主题和写作规范

## 适用场景

- 修订机械化、模板化的论文表达
- 用真实文献增强文献综述和理论部分
- 根据用户提供的数据、草稿和参考文献生成论文段落
- 将 CopyKiller 报告中的标记段落映射回当前论文稿
- 下载后改造成其他学科或具体论文主题的专用 skill

## 使用方法

在 Codex 或兼容 AI Agent 中：

1. 将本文件夹放入 skills 目录。
2. 让 agent 使用 `thesis-ai-naturalizer`。
3. 提供论文草稿、CopyKiller 报告、研究主题、数据和参考文献。
4. 提交前人工核对所有引用、事实和研究结论。

在其他 agent 中：

1. 让 agent 先阅读 `SKILL.md`。
2. 再按 `references/` 里的流程执行。
3. 根据自己的论文提供文献、数据和格式要求。

## 私人化定制

你可以下载后改造成其他领域或论文主题的版本：

- 将 LIS 领域锚点替换成目标学科术语
- 用自己的已核验文献库替换 `references/verified_literature.md`
- 根据目标语言和学校要求调整写作规则
- 加入自己在修订过程中总结出的高风险表达模式

## 学术诚信

本项目用于合法的学术写作辅助，重点是提升文本清晰度、证据支撑、段落结构和学术表达质量。请勿用于编造文献、伪造数据或歪曲研究结果。
